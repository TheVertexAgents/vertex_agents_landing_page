# Competitive Positioning — Vertex Sentinel

Summary
Vertex Sentinel does not compete with AI trading strategies or LLM frameworks. It competes with blind key delegation and black-box execution. Our wedge is **verifiable enforcement at the execution layer**, not prompt-time advisories or centralized custodian risk controls.

Direct comparisons
- AI agent security vendors / prompt guardrails: they filter inputs; we enforce outcomes on-chain
- Smart-contract wallets / DeFi guardrails: they protect EOAs; we protect agentic intents and cross-system execution
- AI trading bots: they optimize returns; we make execution auditable, fail-closed, and non-custodial
- x402 / payments rails: they move value; we authorize *whether* that value should move

Differentiators
- non-custodial: EIP-712 signing replaces private-key handoff
- fail-closed by default: any validation failure halts execution
- on-chain policy: circuit breakers, deadlines, and identity checks live in smart contracts
- verifiable trail: every intent, signature, and decision is auditable
- protocol-level fit: MCP-native, ERC-8004-aligned, exchange-agnostic

Why we win early
- enterprise buyers want controls, not just better models
- regulators and insurers increasingly require verifiable authorization
- most “agent security” is advisory; Sentinel is enforcement
- public proof reduces procurement friction: audits become product demos
